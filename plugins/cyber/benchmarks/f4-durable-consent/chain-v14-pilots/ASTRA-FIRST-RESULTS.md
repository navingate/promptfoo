# F4 defense: Astra-first diagnostic screen

Date: 2026-11-17. Branch: `codex/f4-astra-pilots`. These are development diagnostics, not a frozen benchmark calibration or a floor claim. No GLM requests were made in this screen.

## Question

Can any of the five proposed F4 defensive-chain directions make a strong model struggle for a security reason before we spend effort building a full candidate? The options were active containment, multi-user campaign response, token/session lifetime, authorization drift, and detection-to-response.

## Decision

None of the five small probes showed an Astra struggle. The one executable chain probe was solved. **Do not run GLM yet.** The one-shot probes cannot rule out harder interactive versions of options 2–5, but they do rule out treating the exposed decision itself as frontier-hard.

The next candidate should combine the coherent response pressures from options 2 and 3: a partial initial alert, a discoverable second affected principal, mixed authorized and unauthorized users of one client, distinct grant/token/resource-session controls, and outcome replay after actions. The defender must discover scope, choose narrow actions, and verify that protected access is closed while approved work continues. This needs its own construct review and model-free reference before any scored Astra run. Avoid making it harder by hiding decisive evidence, tightening budgets, or adding arbitrary syntax.
