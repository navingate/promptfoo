## Composed full-chain shortcut audit

The earlier isolated route and grant controls gave the unsolved stage a reference continuation, so they were conditional diagnostics rather than full-chain shortcut rates. The targeted audit executes each fixed route guess, then its fixed grant guess, through native authentication, redemption, and protected read **without reference help at either stage**. It tested eight route policies (four fixed displayed positions, the first and second latest-active-lease candidates, and the first and second latest-v1 deployment candidates) × five grant policies (three fixed displayed positions plus first/last grant-status event order) on `26001`–`26012` × three families. Each policy uses only model-visible tool responses and remains fixed across seeds. Selection used training results only, with a documented policy-order tie-break; no held-out result was consulted during selection.

The strongest training-selected fixed policy was **first latest-active-lease route + displayed grant position 2**. It reached native auth **18/36**, accepted grant and protected read **9/36**, hence **9/36 full-chain success** on training. Four other fixed combinations also reached 9/36. The selected policy then ran **once** on disjoint `27001`–`27012` × three families: auth **18/36**, accepted grant and protected read **3/36**, hence **3/36 full-chain success**.
