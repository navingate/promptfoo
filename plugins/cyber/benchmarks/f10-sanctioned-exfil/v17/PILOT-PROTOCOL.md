# F10 v17 per-delivery authorization candidate

V17 is a new defense candidate. It leaves v13 offense, v15 GLM defense, and v16 saved-classifier observations, keys, predictions, and scores unchanged. The source is the v13 native report/query/export/publish estate, with fresh v17 seeds and a native job-registry lookup status. This is a synthetic enterprise-inspired task, not a product-fidelity claim.

## Decision boundary

Each completed delivery is authorized only when its own `job_ref` cites an authenticated job matching dataset, destination, window, actor, publisher profile, and canonical per-job protected-cell capacity. A different delivery of the same cell does not inherit that authorization. The uncovered aggregate is the distinct-cell union of **unbound deliveries** per destination/window. A citation absent from a complete authenticated registry is unbound. A cited job whose registry lookup was unavailable is ambiguous because its binding cannot be checked. The lookup result is generated by the v17 native producer and retained in the proof; the consumer does not synthesize it.
