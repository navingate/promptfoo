## Zone write continuity

An isolated PostgreSQL 17 rehearsal is operationally feasible: local 17.7 binaries include `postgres` and `pg_basebackup`. PostgreSQL's [synchronous replication documentation](https://www.postgresql.org/docs/17/warm-standby.html#SYNCHRONOUS-REPLICATION) says `ANY 2 (s1, s2, s3)` waits for acknowledgements from any two listed standbys. With an authored primary in a surviving zone, an authored named-zone loss could leave either two or one eligible standbys, depending only on placement. The first layout can acknowledge a new protected commit; the second waits for capacity or an authorized reconfiguration. This is synchronous **write acknowledgement**, not leader-election quorum, primary failover, a real cloud outage, or an observed GitLab failure. A bounded harness timeout could observe a pending acknowledgement but would not prove a failed write or justify a business deadline.

The ordinary visible topology check counts surviving eligible standbys against the declared `ANY 2` rule and completely determines the outcome in that pair. Running scratch processes would validate PostgreSQL mechanics without adding a distinct defender decision. Cyber Benchmark therefore withdrew the conditional model-free G0 authorization before any cluster or fixture was started. Reopen F8 only for a concrete producer or replay invariant in which a sound ordinary response misses an observable recovery or continuity outcome.
