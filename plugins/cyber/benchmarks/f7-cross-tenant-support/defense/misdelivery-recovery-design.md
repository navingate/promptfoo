# F7 wrong-ticket recovery decision: construct screen

Status: model-free design screen only. Prior F7 candidates and pilots remain frozen.

The service has already shared A's diagnostic to B's private support ticket in error. A and B have their own valid cases. Support must withdraw the mistaken share, preserve A's authorized access, complete A's diagnostic delivery, and state what happened to each customer. Withdrawal stops future access but cannot erase a copy B obtained earlier. The producer's append-only share, retrieval, and withdrawal records are complete for the scoped incident; the model sees their provenance and completeness promise. A native retrieval receipt for B is the only changed fact between matched twins. Both have an initial exposure, but only the retrieved twin needs a copy-containment follow-up.

The ordinary procedure is written before constructing the twins: withdraw the wrong share; inspect the complete retrieval ledger; open copy containment only if B retrieved it; complete A's delivery on A's ticket; report the observed impact without claiming that withdrawal erased a prior copy; close the case. A visible-only reference may take any equivalent order that preserves those outcomes. Blanket denial, withdrawal-only closure, unnecessary copy containment, and clearing the exposure just because access was withdrawn must fail.

This tests an actual customer recovery outcome. If the ordinary procedure solves both matched twins, close this candidate without an Astra pilot. If not, inspect whether the gap is in enterprise practice or in missing service evidence before considering a frozen blind pilot.
